Assistify is a WhatsApp automation platform built for liner shipping companies. It empowers sales executives to instantly check slot rates, route availability, vessel schedules, and container inventory—while engaging customers faster and managing communication at scale.

From rate inquiry to confirmed booking—instantly on WhatsApp.

Schedule A Demo →

⚓ Key Features

  • 💰 Instant WhatsApp rates: Reps find and share slot rates inside chat window in seconds.
  • 🚢 Vessel schedules: Real-time access to vessel routing and schedule availability.
  • 📦 Live inventory: Real-time container inventory updates at regional depots.
  • 🔄 Lead follow-ups: Automated reminders and workflows for active quotes.
  • 🔌 ERP Integration: Seamless link with your core enterprise shipping databases.
