Extraction is just the beginning. Singularity gives you full operational control over your extracted data.

1 Extract from Anything

Upload images, PDFs, Excel files, or documents. Singularity's AI agents read everything—tables, text, complex layouts, even scanned documents. No manual data entry. No reformatting. Just clean, structured tariff data ready to use.

2 Edit Everything

Unlike basic OCR tools that give you static output, Singularity puts you in complete control. Re-arrange and edit files directly in your browser:

  • Edit rows — Fix, update, or refine any extracted field
  • Add rows — Insert new tariff entries seamlessly
  • Bulk change — Update hundreds of rows with one action
  • Custom fields — Add the data points your business needs

Every carrier, every country, every tariff file is different. Singularity lets you define custom extraction rules so the AI knows exactly what to look for—every time. Train it once. Use it forever. Perfect extraction, every upload.

Bulk classify thousands of products in seconds. Run bulk AI operations with natural language commands—just describe what you want, and Singularity does the rest.
