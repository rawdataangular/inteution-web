Mid-Senior QA Engineer

2-3 Years Experience
Opening Date: December 5, 2025

🚢 Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Mid-Senior Quality Assurance Engineer to join our core product team. You will be instrumental in ensuring the reliability, performance, and accuracy of our specialized software platform used by NVOCCs and liner companies worldwide. Your expertise will directly impact the speed and profitability of global container logistics.

🔑 Key Responsibilities

  • Test Strategy & Execution: Design, develop, and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases for new features, focusing on our core modules (Container Tracking, Vessel Scheduling, Documentation, and Accounting).
  • Manual & Exploratory Testing: Conduct thorough manual and exploratory testing to identify complex bugs and usability issues that automated scripts may miss.
  • Automation Development (Advantage): Develop, maintain, and execute automated test scripts using standard frameworks (e.g., Selenium, Playwright, Cypress) for regression and functional testing.
  • Defect Management: Identify, document, prioritize, and track defects through their life cycle using tools like Jira or Azure DevOps.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with product managers and development teams in an Agile/Scrum environment to understand requirements and acceptance criteria.
  • Domain Focus: Ensure all features accurately adhere to international shipping and accounting standards.

🌟 Required Qualifications

  • Experience: 2–3 years of hands-on experience in Quality Assurance (QA) engineering.
  • Technical Skills: Proficiency with testing methodologies, creating test plans, and strong SQL skills for backend data validation.
  • Critical Thinking: Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen eye for detail.
  • Communication: Strong written and verbal communication skills.

🌐 Advantageous Experience (Big Plus)

Candidates with experience in any of the following areas will be strongly considered:

  • Shipping & Logistics Domain Knowledge: Direct working experience or strong knowledge of shipping operations, ocean cargo movement, NVOCC processes, container management, or port operations.
  • Test Automation: Proven experience in building and maintaining test automation frameworks and scripts (e.g., using Python, Java, or JavaScript).
  • API Testing: Experience with API testing tools (e.g., Postman) to validate backend services and data flow.

🚀 Why Join Our Team?

We are a domain-expert led company building the operating system for the next generation of global shipping. You will have the opportunity to:

  • Work on complex, real-world problems that directly impact international trade.
  • Be part of a collaborative culture that values data accuracy and technical excellence.
  • Gain deep domain knowledge in the high-growth logistics technology sector.

Application Instructions

Interested candidates should submit their resume and a brief cover letter outlining their experience in QA and any relevant logistics knowledge to info@inteution.com
